\subsection*{Intersections d'une droite et d'un cercle}

\bigskip
Droite.
\[ y = ax + b \]

\medskip
Cercle.
\[ x^2 + y^2 - r^2 = 0 \]

\medskip
Intersections.
\[ (a^2 + 1)x^2 + 2abx + b^2 - r^2 = 0 \]

\medskip
Discriminant.
\[ \Delta = (2ab)^2 - 4(a^2 + 1)(b^2 - r^2) \]

\medskip
Solutions (si \( \Delta \geq 0 \)).
\[ x = \frac{-2ab \pm \sqrt{\Delta}}{2(a^2 + 1)} \]
